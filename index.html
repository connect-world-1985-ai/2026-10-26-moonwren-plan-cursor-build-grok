Features

Original night market

A wren, a crescent moon and glowing lanterns, all drawn in code. No borrowed art.

Music made in code

A short pentatonic tune and every sound effect are synthesised live in your browser.

English and 中文

Follows your browser language, with a one-tap switch that remembers your choice.

Desktop and phone

Keyboard, mouse or touch. The playfield fits any screen without scrolling.

Pause and mute

Pause at any moment. One mute control silences the music and effects together.

Best score saved

Your best run stays on this device. No account, no tracking, no network calls.

How to play

Fly

  • Tap, click, or press Space or ↑ to flap.
  • Hold the gap between the lanterns. Each gate you pass scores 1.
  • Touch a lantern or the eaves and the gate closes. Tap again to perch, then fly again.
  • The gates speed up and narrow as your score climbs.

Controls

  • Space / ↑ flap, start, perch again
  • P / Esc pause and resume
  • M mute or unmute music and effects
  • The 中文 / EN button switches the language.
